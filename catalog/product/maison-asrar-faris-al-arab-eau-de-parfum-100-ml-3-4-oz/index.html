Faris Al Arab is a bold and refined masculine fragrance that blends fresh citrus, aromatic spices, and smooth woods into a modern Middle Eastern scent. Faris Al Arab is perfect for men who enjoy fresh woody fragrances with a spicy Middle Eastern touch and strong everyday versatility.
