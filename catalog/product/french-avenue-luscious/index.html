Description
Luscious is a rich and indulgent gourmand fragrance built around creamy pistachio, warm spices, and deep vanilla sweetness. The scent feels luxurious, addictive, and unique, offering a nutty dessert-like profile with a refined and elegant twist. The opening introduces a smooth blend of pistachio, bergamot, and cardamom, creating a creamy, slightly fresh, and aromatic first impression. Luscious is perfect for women who enjoy gourmand fragrances with a nutty, creamy, and slightly spiced character.
