Description
Khamrah Dukhan by Lattafa is a deep, smoky, spicy-sweet, resinous take on the original Khamrah DNA — darker, richer, and more intense. It opens with warm cinnamon, spicy nutmeg, and sweet dates, giving a gourmand-spiced introduction. The heart develops into smoky incense, dried fruits, caramel warmth, and a hint of boozy richness, creating a thick, luxurious aroma. The base settles into oud smoke, vanilla, tonka, praline, amber woods, and musk, creating a bold and long-lasting trail with a signature “dukhan” (smoke) depth. This is ideal for customers who love sweet-spicy, smoky, Middle-Eastern gourmands with strong projection and performance.
