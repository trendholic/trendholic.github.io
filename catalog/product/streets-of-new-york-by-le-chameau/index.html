Streets Of New York | Eau De Parfum 85ml | by Le Chameau Top notes: Bergamot, Flowers, Coriander Middle notes: Apple, Ambroxan, Vanilla Base notes: Tonka Bean, Ambergris, Woodsy Notes Streets Of New York opens with a vibrant blend of bergamot, soft floral notes, and a hint of coriander, painting a picture of early morning light hitting the skyline — fresh, energetic, and full of promise. The heart of the fragrance reveals a modern, addictive twist: crisp apple fused with smooth ambroxan and vanilla, evoking the buzz of city life — where concrete meets charm, and movement meets mystery. As it settles, a warm trail of tonka bean, ambergris, and woodsy notes emerges, creating a deep and lasting impression, much like the memory of a twilight walk through Manhattan’s lit-up avenues. Streets Of New York is bold yet refined, vibrant yet grounded — made for those who thrive in the pulse of the city and leave a mark wherever they go.
