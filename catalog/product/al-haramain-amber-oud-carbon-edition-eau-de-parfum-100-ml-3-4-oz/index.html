Description
Amber Oud Carbon Edition is a fresh, aromatic fragrance that blends citrus brightness with green herbal notes and a smooth woody amber base. The scent feels clean, modern, and masculine-leaning, often compared to high-end fresh designer fragrances with a slightly deeper twist. The opening introduces a crisp combination of bergamot, lavender, and rosemary, creating a bright, herbal, and slightly citrusy freshness. The heart develops into sea notes, geranium, and sage, adding a clean aquatic feel with aromatic depth and a slightly green character. The base settles into moss, vetiver, amber, and cedar, leaving a smooth, woody, and slightly earthy trail that feels fresh yet grounded and long-lasting. Amber Oud Carbon Edition is perfect for those who enjoy clean, fresh fragrances with a green woody backbone and strong everyday versatility.
