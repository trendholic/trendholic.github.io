Description
Boulevard of New York by Le Chameau is a tribute to the heartbeat of the city—elegant, energetic, and effortlessly modern. This gourmand-woody fragrance evokes the charm of Manhattan mornings and the warmth of afternoon strolls along its iconic avenues. Designed for the sophisticated soul with a taste for both indulgence and depth. Top Note: Roasted Coffee Beans Heart Notes: Praline, Rose Base Notes: Oakmoss, Amber, Cedar This fragrance opens with the bold aroma of freshly roasted coffee beans, awakening the senses like a brisk morning in NYC. As the scent develops, the heart melts into a delicate yet indulgent pairing of sweet praline and blooming rose—evoking a café rendezvous on a bustling boulevard. The dry-down is a refined blend of earthy oakmoss, golden amber, and smooth cedarwood, creating a grounded finish that lingers with understated power. Boulevard of New York is ideal for those who appreciate sophistication with a twist of sweetness—a modern signature scent that transitions effortlessly from day to night.
