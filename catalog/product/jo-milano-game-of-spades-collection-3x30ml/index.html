The Jo Milano Game of Spades 3-Piece Set is a luxury fragrance collection featuring three of the brand’s most iconic scents: Wildcard, Full-House, and Royale . Together, this set provides versatility for different moods, occasions, and seasons — from fresh daily wear to bold evening statements. Perfect for collectors, gifting, or anyone looking to experience the Game of Spades collection in one premium set.
