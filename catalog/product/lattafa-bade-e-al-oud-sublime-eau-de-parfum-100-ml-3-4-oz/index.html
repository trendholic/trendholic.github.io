Description
Bade’e Al Oud Sublime by Lattafa is a sweet, fruity, gourmand-woody fragrance that blends juicy fruits with smooth oud and warm amber. It opens with ripe plum, apple, and fruity sweetness, creating a bright, mouthwatering first impression. The heart develops into floral notes and soft woods, balancing the sweetness with elegance and depth. The base settles into oud, vanilla, amber, and musk, delivering a smooth, cozy, long-lasting trail that feels addictive and comforting rather than smoky. This fragrance is ideal for customers who love sweet fruity gourmands with a refined Middle Eastern touch and excellent performance.
