Description
Swiss Arabian Shaghaf Vanilla Toffee is a rich gourmand fragrance built around buttery toffee, creamy vanilla, and warm dessert-like sweetness. The scent feels indulgent and comforting, blending coffee, caramelized sugar, and smooth woods into a warm, addictive composition. The opening introduces buttery toffee and rich Arabic coffee, creating a sweet and slightly roasted aroma that immediately feels warm and inviting. The heart reveals vanilla, walnut pastry, dates, milk, and cardamom, adding creamy sweetness with a soft nutty spice. The base settles into brown sugar, Madagascar vanilla, benzoin, tonka bean, musk, and woods, producing a deep caramel-vanilla trail that feels cozy and long-lasting. Shaghaf Vanilla Toffee is perfect for lovers of ultra-sweet gourmand fragrances that smell warm, creamy, and dessert-like.
