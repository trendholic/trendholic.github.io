Description
III Thriller is a dark and intense extrait de parfum that blends bright spices, smoky leather, and creamy woods into a bold niche-style fragrance. The scent feels powerful, mysterious, and luxurious with a rich progression from fresh spicy notes into a deep woody amber dry-down. The opening introduces lemon, bergamot, cardamom, saffron, elemi, and pepper, creating a vibrant burst of citrus and warm spice with an energetic aromatic edge. The heart develops into leather, incense, labdanum, clary sage, and tonka bean, adding smoky depth and a smooth resinous warmth that gives the fragrance its bold signature character. The base settles into sandalwood, vanilla, amber, benzoin, cashmeran, and musk, leaving a creamy woody trail that feels warm, addictive, and long-lasting on the skin. III Thriller is perfect for those who enjoy bold woody-spicy fragrances with smoky leather depth and a rich luxurious dry-down.
