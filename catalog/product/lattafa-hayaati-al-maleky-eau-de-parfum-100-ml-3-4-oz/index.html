Description
Hayaati Al Maleky is a bold and energetic fragrance that blends fresh citrus, warm spices, and smooth woody accords into a modern masculine scent. The fragrance feels powerful, versatile, and refined while maintaining a youthful fresh-spicy character. The opening introduces bergamot, pink pepper, and ginger, creating a bright citrus freshness with warm spicy energy and aromatic depth. The heart develops into lavender, incense, and woody notes, adding smoky elegance balanced by smooth masculine freshness. The base settles into amber, musk, cedarwood, and vetiver, leaving a warm, woody, and long-lasting trail that feels clean, modern, and sophisticated. Hayaati Al Maleky is perfect for men who enjoy fresh spicy fragrances with smoky woods and a strong masculine presence.
