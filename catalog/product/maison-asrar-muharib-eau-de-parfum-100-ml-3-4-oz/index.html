Description
Muharib is a bold and masculine fragrance that blends warm spices, smoky woods, and rich leather into a powerful modern scent. The fragrance feels intense, refined, and confident while maintaining a smooth luxurious warmth throughout the wear. The opening introduces spicy accords with fresh citrus nuances, creating an energetic and commanding first impression with aromatic depth. The heart develops into leather, woods, and herbal notes, adding a smoky masculine richness balanced by smooth aromatic freshness. The base settles into amber, musk, patchouli, and sandalwood, leaving a warm, woody, and long-lasting trail that feels strong, elegant, and sophisticated. Muharib is perfect for men who enjoy bold woody leather fragrances with warm spices and a refined Middle Eastern character.
