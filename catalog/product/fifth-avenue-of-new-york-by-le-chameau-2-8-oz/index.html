Description
Fifth Avenue Of New York by Le Chameau is a deliciously indulgent fragrance inspired by the glamour and sweetness of life on one of the world’s most iconic streets. Designed for those who love to stand out with confidence and femininity, this scent is a chic gourmand fantasy bottled in elegance. Top Notes: Strawberry, Raspberry, Coconut, Pineapple Heart Notes: Burnt Sugar, Whipped Cream, Vanilla Base Notes: Amber, Musk, Tonka Bean This captivating scent opens with a mouthwatering medley of ripe strawberry, tangy raspberry, creamy coconut, and tropical pineapple—setting a playful and fruity tone. The heart melts into a delectable dessert of burnt sugar, whipped cream, and lush vanilla, evoking the scent of a luxurious patisserie on a sunny New York afternoon. As it dries down, the base of warm amber, soft musk, and cozy tonka bean leaves a smooth, sensual finish that lingers with sophistication. Ideal for those who adore sweet, flirty fragrances with depth and lasting power, Fifth Avenue Of New York is your perfect scent for turning heads and making everyday moments feel indulgent.
