Description
Village Of New York by Le Chameau is a radiant and feminine fragrance inspired by the charm, creativity, and bohemian spirit of Greenwich Village. Lush fruits, delicate florals, and warm gourmand notes come together in a scent that feels effortlessly stylish—perfect for those who embrace individuality with grace and flair. Top Notes: Litchi, Cassis, Mandarin Orange Heart Notes: Water Lily, Peony, Jasmine Base Notes: Ambroxan, Praline, Musk, Vanilla, Oak Moss The fragrance begins with a playful and juicy blend of litchi, cassis, and mandarin orange—fresh, sweet, and slightly tart, like a sunlit morning in the city. The heart blooms with soft water lily, velvety peony, and luminous jasmine, creating a bouquet that’s both modern and romantic. As it dries down, a creamy base of praline, vanilla, and musk is grounded by earthy oak moss and the sensual depth of ambroxan—leaving a soft yet confident trail. Village Of New York is a versatile scent made for daydreamers, artists, and free spirits. It’s perfect for daytime elegance, brunch dates, or strolls through the city—with a scent that lingers like a beautiful memory.
