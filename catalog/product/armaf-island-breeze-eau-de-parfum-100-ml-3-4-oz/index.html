Description
Armaf Island Breeze is a refreshing aquatic-fresh fragrance that captures the feeling of a cool ocean wind on a warm tropical day. Island Breeze is perfect for those who want a clean, ocean-fresh signature scent that works easily for everyday wear and warm weather.
