Description
Rasasi Hawas for Him is a powerful, modern aquatic fragrance known for its fresh sweetness, strong projection, and mass appeal. It combines fruity brightness with aquatic freshness and a warm musky base, making it one of the most recognizable men’s scents in its category. The opening bursts with crisp apple, bergamot, lemon, and cinnamon, delivering a fresh yet slightly sweet and spicy introduction. The heart brings in watery aquatic notes, plum, orange blossom, and cardamom, creating a smooth, clean, and addictive freshness. The base settles into ambergris, musk, driftwood, and patchouli, providing warmth, longevity, and a masculine backbone that lasts for hours. Hawas for Him is ideal for men who want a compliment-pulling, confident fragrance that performs exceptionally well in warm weather.
